# Changelog

## Unreleased

### Added
- Classic look for Blizzard's Cooldown Manager: square 2004-style icons with a thin dark edge, a square cooldown sweep, smaller buff timers, and Classic buff bars. Positions stay in Edit Mode and everything works in combat. On by default; /ccm to switch it off.
- Classic Bars: your own bars of square Classic icons, set up in /ccm > Classic Bars. Tick spells from your spellbook onto the Cooldowns or Utility bar; each spell shows once at your highest rank and switches when you train a new rank. Cooldown sweep and countdown in combat, greyed while cooling down, red out of range, blue when low on mana, and a gold edge when a reactive ability (Overpower, Revenge, Execute, Riposte, Counterattack, Mongoose Bite) is ready. Per bar: icon size, spacing, hide when ready and only in combat. Unlock to drag them anywhere. Off by default.
- Buffs bar: tick Buff on spells that put a buff on you (Thorns, Mark of the Wild, Rejuvenation) or on your class procs (Clearcasting, Shadow Trance and more). Each shows while it's on you, with time left and stacks, in combat too, using the game's own secure buff display. Active buffs sit side by side in your order, centred, with no gaps; or tick Show missing buffs greyed to keep every buff in its own spot. A spell can be on a cooldown bar and the Buffs bar at once. Buffs from other players count at any rank, and group versions (Gift of the Wild, Prayer of Fortitude, Greater Blessings) count as the same buff. Racial buffs match too, including racials whose buff has its own spell ID (Walk on Air).
- Trinkets and potions: your trinket slots and usable bag items can go on the Cooldowns or Utility bar, with their cooldown and how many you have left. Tick Show items to list them.
- Add by name or spell ID: put spells outside your spellbook on a bar, like another class's buff on you. Matching names appear as you type.
- Show spell names: optional names under each bar's icons.
- Show all ranks: list every rank you know as its own row and put a lower rank on a bar, for casting it on purpose (a cheap rank 1 Moonfire, say). It stays on that rank; the normal row always follows your highest.
- /ccm settings window: one screen with Spellbook, Bars and Settings side by side, in a flat dark style. Pick its accent colour: orange, blue, teal, purple or green. Clear a whole bar with two clicks. Also listed under Options > AddOns.
- Settings backup: your choices are also kept in the game's own settings, so they come back if the client forgets addon settings after a restart.
