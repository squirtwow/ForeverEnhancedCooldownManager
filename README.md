# Classic Cooldown Manager

Classic 2004 look for Blizzard's built-in **Cooldown Manager** on
**World of Warcraft: Forever**. Works on its own, and sits right at home next
to EraUI.

## Classic look
- Square 2004-style icons with a thin dark edge, instead of the modern rounded
  frames, for Essential and Utility cooldowns and tracked buffs
- A square Classic cooldown sweep; out-of-range spells turn red, as in Classic
- Tracked buff bars with the Classic bar texture, a dark background and Classic
  fonts
- Visual only: Blizzard still decides what shows and when, positions and sizes
  stay in Edit Mode, and everything works in combat
- On by default; switch it off in `/ccm` and reload to restore Blizzard's look

## Commands
- `/ccm` opens the settings, also found under Options > AddOns

Blizzard's Cooldown Manager must be on: Options > Gameplay > Advanced Options >
Enable Cooldown Manager.
