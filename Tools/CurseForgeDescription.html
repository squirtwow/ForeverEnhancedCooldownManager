⏱️ Forever Enhanced Cooldown Manager

💬 Support, bugs & ideas: join our Discord to get help, report issues and suggest features.

A cleaner look for Blizzard's Cooldown Manager and Personal Resource Display on World of Warcraft: Forever, plus cooldown, buff and cast bars of your own that stack around your resource display, and a big pulse on screen when a cooldown is ready. Every feature is optional.

 

✦ Highlights

🎨 The look

  • 🔲 A new look for Blizzard's Cooldown Manager: square icons, bold countdowns and Tracked Bars in three designs. Presets restyle it all in one click
  • 🖼️ Borders and shadows round icons or whole bars, with the Font and bar texture you like
  • ⚡ Procs and reactive abilities glow like your action bars, on Riposte, Overpower, Execute and more
  • ⌨️ Keybinds on icons, read from your action bars

❤️ Resource display

  • ❤️ Personal Resource Display: health and power in the same designs, with Health and power text
  • 🔴 Combo points under your energy bar for rogues and cat druids

🧱 Your own bars

  • 🧱 Your own bars: Cooldowns, Utility, Buffs and Debuffs, always at your highest rank
  • 🧭 Layouts: stack your bars round your resource display in one click
  • 🌫️ Show, dim or hide when ready, or while you can't cast it
  • 🏹 Drag items in: trinkets, potions, ammo and your Hearthstone

🛡️ Buffs and debuffs

  • 🔗 One icon for one-at-a-time spells: Seals, Auras, Aspects, Curses and more share one icon
  • 💡 Glow when missing: spot a buff that fell off, or a debuff missing from your target
  • 🔢 Count ticks: a debuff counts its ticks like stacks

✨ Cast bar, pulse and raids

  • ✨ Cast bar with tick marks, latency and a swing timer
  • 💥 Cooldown pulse: a big icon on screen the moment a cooldown is ready
  • ⏳ Raid Timers: pull countdown, raid warnings and boss casts in your bar design

👥 Profiles and help

  • 👥 Profiles per character, role or talents, and share them as text
  • 🎓 A quick tour, plus a ? walkthrough on every page

➕ …and plenty more in the settings: click the minimap button, or type /ccm

 

 

 

✦ Getting started

  1. Log in: the settings open by themselves the first time, with a quick tour you can skip
  2. After that, click the minimap button, or find the settings under Options > AddOns (typing /ccm works too)
  3. For your own bars, tick Use my bars on the General page, then add spells on each bar's page
  4. Pick a layout on the Layout page to stack them around your Personal Resource Display

Blizzard's Cooldown Manager must be on for its new look: the settings tell you if it's off and can turn it on.
