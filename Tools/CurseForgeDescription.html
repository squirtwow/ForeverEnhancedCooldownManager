⏱️ Forever Enhanced Cooldown Manager

💬 Support, bugs & ideas: join our Discord to get help, report issues and suggest features.

A cleaner look for Blizzard's Cooldown Manager and Personal Resource Display on World of Warcraft: Forever, plus cooldown, buff and cast bars of your own that stack around your resource display. Every feature is optional.

✦ Highlights

  • 🔲 A new look for Blizzard's Cooldown Manager: square icons with big, bold countdown numbers, and Tracked Bars in three designs (Glass, Split and Outline) and six colours, or a colour of their own for any bar
  • ❤️ Personal Resource Display: its health and power bars in the same designs, in Blizzard's colours or yours. The extra mana bar some specs get hides in caster form and is half size in forms
  • 🔴 Combo points under your energy bar for rogues and druids in cat form, which Forever's display leaves out
  • 🧱 Your own bars: Cooldowns, Utility, Buffs and Debuffs. Tick spells or drag them in from your spellbook; each spell shows once, at your highest rank
  • 🧭 Layouts: one click stacks your bars around your Personal Resource Display, as a pyramid, wide rows, sides and more. Change any row, drag icons between rows, take a bar out, or Reset
  • 🎯 Debuff search: only your class's debuffs are listed, and you pick the spot in the Debuffs row
  • ✨ Cast bar: your own, right under your resource display, with the spell's icon, name and time left. Blizzard's hides while it's on
  • 🖼️ Borders and shadows round each icon or round whole bars
  • 👥 Profiles: share one across characters and classes, and each character only shows its own spells. Use on all characters sets it everywhere
  • 💾 Backed up: your settings are also kept in the game's own settings, in case the game loses them
  • 🎓 A quick tour the first time you log in: it opens each page and points at the basics. Skip it, or take it again any time
  • 🗺️ Minimap button: click it for the settings, drag it round the minimap, or hide it

✦ Getting started

  1. Log in: the settings open by themselves the first time, with a quick tour you can skip
  2. After that, click the minimap button or type /ccm (also under Options > AddOns)
  3. For your own bars, tick Use my bars on the General page, then add spells on each bar's page
  4. Pick a layout on the Layout page to stack them around your Personal Resource Display

Blizzard's Cooldown Manager must be on for its new look: /ccm tells you if it's off and can turn it on.
